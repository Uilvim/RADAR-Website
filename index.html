About

A commons built on community.

RADAR brings Rice faculty, students, and staff from across campus together to explore how to responsibly use AI, data, and computation to advance research. Technical staff from Fondren Library and the Center for Research Computing are co-located here to build services and resources side by side.

01

Community

A shared home for communities of practice, learning communities, and cross-campus collaboration.

02

Flexibility

Movable furniture and reconfigurable rooms adapt to meetings, classes, and events.

03

Openness

Open to aligned groups across campus to gather, book, and build together.

04

Sustainability

Natural and recycled materials, biophilic design, daylight, and programming on AI and the environment.

    Sustainability

    Good for research. Good for the planet.

    Overlooking the rain garden, RADAR practices environmental stewardship in both its programming and its physical presence.

    Programming

    • Hosting the AI and the Environment Learning Community
    • Events and workshops on climate AI

    Physical space

    • Natural or recycled materials
    • Reduced energy consumption
    • Biophilic design
    • Indoor air quality and natural light prioritized
