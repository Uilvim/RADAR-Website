Space Needs for the RADAR Commons
October 2026
Vision for the Rice AI, Data and Research (RADAR) Commons
The Rice AI, Data and Research (RADAR) Commons will bring together Rice faculty, students, and staff from across campus to explore how to responsibly use AI, data, and computation to advance research. In the RADAR Commons, technical staff from Fondren Library and the Center for Research Computing will consult with researchers on best practices for using AI and data in research, host communities of practice and events, and teach hands-on workshops. Key design principles include community, flexibility, openness, and sustainability. Operating as a community space, RADAR will enable aligned groups (such as Rice Digital Learning and the Ken Kennedy Information Institute) to book its open gathering area (room 200) and large and small conference rooms (rooms 201 and 200B). Most of the furniture will be easy to move, and we will reconfigure the space to accommodate meetings and classes. By co-locating offices for library and CRC staff, the RADAR Commons will facilitate collaboration and creativity in developing services and resources in support of responsible AI in research. Located on the second floor mezzanine of Fondren Library and overlooking the rain garden, the RADAR Commons will foster sustainability and environmental stewardship through its programming (such as by hosting the AI and the Environment Learning Community and events on climate AI) and its physical presence (such as by using natural or recycled materials, reducing energy consumption, incorporating biophilic design, and prioritizing indoor air quality and the use of natural light). We will use screens strategically throughout the space to facilitate collaboration and learning. 

The RADAR Commons will feature:
Fondren 200: This large (1970 square foot), open space will serve several functions. For consultations, we will provide one large and two smaller huddle tables with screens. To encourage collaboration and conversation, we will provide soft seating along the glass wall overlooking the first floor. We will retain a few of the small circular tables from the current CAPC space to be used for consultations not requiring shared screens; students may also use these tables for individual or group study. We will mount a large screen on the wall on the opposite side of Fondren 201 that can be used for larger workshops and events. We will have a small stock of tables and chairs on wheels that we can move into the space for events and workshops. 
Fondren 201: This large conference room will support hybrid meetings, hands-on workshops, and gatherings. This space will have a large screen against the south wall and smaller screens on the left and right sides. It will provide access to a 360-degree video conferencing system (like the Owl) for hybrid meetings. The furniture will be reconfigurable based on the intended use (e.g. meeting, class, lecture). 
Fondren 200A: Office for a library staff member.
Fondren 200B: Small conference room. This space will be used for meetings (for up to 6 people) and consultations. We will retain the existing monitor, table, and chairs in the space. 
Fondren 200C: This two-person office will likely be used by CRC staff.
Fondren 200D: Office for one or two library staff members/graduate consultants, possibly using a hot-desking strategy. 
Fondren 200E: Office for library staff member.
Fondren 200F: Storage room. Shelving will be needed.
