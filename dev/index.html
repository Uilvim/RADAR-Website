About

A commons built on community.

RADAR brings Rice faculty, students, and staff from across campus together to explore how to responsibly use AI, data, and computation to advance research. Technical staff from Fondren Library and the Center for Research Computing are co-located here to build services and resources side by side.

01

Community

A shared home for communities of practice, learning communities, and cross-campus collaboration.

02

Flexibility

Movable furniture and reconfigurable rooms adapt to meetings, classes, and events.

03

Openness

Open to aligned groups across campus to gather, book, and build together.

04

Sustainability

Natural and recycled materials, biophilic design, daylight, and programming on AI and the environment.

        FAQ

        Frequently asked questions

        What is the RADAR Commons?

        RADAR (Rice AI, Data and Research) brings Rice faculty, students, and staff together to explore how to responsibly use AI, data, and computation to advance research.

        Where is it located?

        RADAR is on the 2nd floor mezzanine of Fondren Library at Rice University.

        Who staffs RADAR?

        Technical staff from Fondren Library and the Center for Research Computing are co-located here to build services and resources side by side.

        When are office hours?

        AI & Research Data Office Hours run Tuesdays from 10:00 am to 12:00 pm and 1:00 pm to 3:00 pm on Zoom.

        Who can book the space?

        Aligned groups across campus can book the open gathering area (200), the large conference room (201), and the small conference room (200B). Use the request form to get started.

        What is a community of practice?

        A group of peers who explore a shared question together, such as the AI and the Environment Learning Community.

        Get involved

        Bring your group to RADAR.

        Aligned groups can book the open gathering area (200), the large conference room (201), and the small conference room (200B). Tell us what you have in mind.
